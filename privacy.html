Privacy Policy

For Synthia, the private AI assistant operated by CharoAI. Effective 3 September 2026.

1. Who operates Synthia

Synthia is built, hosted, and operated by CharoAI (Chris Charo), Florida, USA. She runs on hardware the operator owns and controls. Questions about this policy go through the contact page.

2. Who uses Synthia

Synthia is a single-user application. The only person who signs in, grants access, or interacts with her is the operator. She is not offered to the public and does not collect information about visitors to this website.

3. What Google user data is accessed

With the operator's authorization through Google OAuth 2.0, Synthia can access the following data in the operator's own Google account:

  • Google Calendar: events, so she can read, create, and update the operator's schedule.
  • Google Tasks: task lists, so she can add, complete, and report on the operator's tasks.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Forms: files the operator owns or has access to, so she can create, read, edit, organize, and share documents on the operator's instruction.
  • Gmail (read): messages, so she can summarize and act on the operator's mail.
  • Gmail (send): the ability to send messages from the operator's address on the operator's instruction.

4. How that data is used

Google user data is used for one purpose: carrying out the requests of the operator who granted access. Data is not sold, not shared with any other person, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles. To fulfil a request, relevant content may be processed by language-model and media-generation services the operator has configured; it is sent only as needed to complete that request and is not used by the operator to train models. Synthia's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored on operator-controlled hardware on a private network, protected by file permissions and network isolation. Data is retained for as long as the operator finds it useful and can be deleted by the operator at any time.

6. Revoking access

The operator can revoke Synthia's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Revocation immediately invalidates her credentials.

7. This website

This page and the rest of charoai.net are static pages. The contact form is handled by Formspree and delivers messages to the operator; nothing else on this site collects personal information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
